## What to Skip

- **Plastic egg cups with painted finishes:** the paint chips after a handful of dishwasher cycles, and you're left with a dingy-looking cup.
- **Egg rings without handles:** anything that requires tongs or a fork to remove from a hot pan is asking for a burn or a broken yolk.
- **"Novelty shaped" egg molds:** the fun shapes (hearts, stars, whatever) almost always stick worse than a simple circle, and you'll stop using them within a week.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What are the best cheap breakfast gadgets to start with?**
If you're just building a breakfast gadget collection, start with an egg ring set since it covers the most ground: eggs, pancakes, and hash browns all in one tool, and it costs under $15.

**Do egg rings actually keep eggs from spreading in the pan?**
Yes, as long as you use a small amount of oil to keep the egg from sticking to the ring itself. Metal rings hold shape better under high heat, while silicone rings are gentler on nonstick pans but can warp slightly if the pan gets too hot.

**Are wooden egg cups dishwasher safe?**
Most aren't, including this one. Wood tends to crack or warp with repeated dishwasher cycles, so a quick hand wash and towel dry is the safer bet to keep it looking good long term.
