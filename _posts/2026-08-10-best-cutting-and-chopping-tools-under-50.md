## What to Skip

- **Ultra-cheap plastic mandolines under $10:** they dull fast and the safety guards are often an afterthought.
- **"All-in-one" choppers with a dozen tiny attachments:** most people use one or two pieces and lose the rest in a drawer.
- **Electric choppers with weak motors:** if a food chopper struggles with a single carrot, it's not saving you time, just adding a new frustration.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What's the best budget alternative to a full knife set?**
A quality all-in-one chopper like the Clever Cutter or a sharp paring knife paired with a sturdy cutting board covers 90% of daily tasks without the cost of a full block set.

**Are bamboo cutting boards better than plastic?**
Bamboo resists warping, holds up better against knife marks over time, and doesn't retain odors the way cheap plastic boards do, making it a solid choice for small prep boards.

**Can an electric cardboard cutter be used for anything besides boxes?**
Yes. Most, including the Ksalltol, handle plastic packaging and leather as well, making them useful for crafters and anyone dealing with frequent deliveries, not just cardboard.
