## What to Skip

- Avoid microwaves without a visible wattage label. Some campus housing offices require 700W or under, and guessing wrong can mean returning it after move-in day.
- Skip anything advertised as "refurbished" at this price point. The margin is already thin, and a used unit with a shorter lifespan isn't worth the savings.
- Don't buy based on color or design alone. A cute retro shell doesn't matter if the door latch loosens after two months of dorm use.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Will a 0.7 cu. ft. microwave fit a dorm room desk?**
Yes, this is the standard size for dorm use. It typically takes up about the same footprint as a small toaster oven and fits on a desk corner, closet shelf, or mini fridge top without dominating the space.

**Is 700 watts enough to actually cook food, or just reheat it?**
700 watts handles reheating, frozen meals, and basic cooking like oatmeal or scrambled eggs just fine. It's slower than a 1000W+ model, so expect to add 15 to 30 extra seconds on package instructions.

**Do dorms actually require a wattage limit on microwaves?**
Many do, and 700 watts is the most common ceiling for shared housing due to electrical circuit limits in older dorm buildings. Check your school's housing guide before buying, since some schools cap it even lower.
