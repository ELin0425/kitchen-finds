## What to Skip

- Avoid waffle makers with no indicator light or audible cue. Guessing when a waffle is done leads to either raw centers or burnt edges.
- Skip anything with plates that aren't removable or dishwasher-safe if you hate hand-scrubbing batter out of grooves.
- Be wary of "Belgian deep-pocket" irons under $50 that skimp on wattage. Low power means long cook times and pale, soggy waffles.
