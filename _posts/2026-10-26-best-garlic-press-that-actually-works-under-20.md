## What to Skip

- Avoid presses with plastic hinges advertised as "heavy duty." They crack within months of regular use and start leaking garlic juice through the seams.
- Skip anything with tiny mesh holes that clog instantly. If a product photo shows a mesh plate with holes smaller than a pinhead, expect to spend more time cleaning than cooking.
- Pass on presses that require you to peel garlic first unless you specifically want that extra step. Several options here, like the Garject Lite, skip it entirely.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the best garlic press under $20?**
The Zulay Kitchen Metal Garlic Press is the strongest all-around pick under $20 thanks to its rust-resistant build and clean mincing action, but the ORBLUE Stainless Steel press is a close runner-up if you want the lowest price without sacrificing durability.

**Do expensive garlic presses actually mince better than cheap ones?**
Not necessarily. Mincing quality depends more on hinge leverage and hole design than price. Several presses on this list perform as well as versions costing twice as much.

**How do I keep my garlic press from getting clogged?**
Rinse it immediately after use before the garlic dries and hardens in the holes. A small brush or an old toothbrush works well for stubborn bits, and most of these presses are dishwasher-safe for a deeper clean.
